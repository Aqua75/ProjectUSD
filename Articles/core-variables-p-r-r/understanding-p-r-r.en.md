# The Heart of ProjectUSD – A Clear Explanation of P, R and r

## Introduction

The three variables **P**, **R** and **r** form the foundation of price stability in ProjectUSD.  
They are easy to understand but economically deep – and this combination is what makes the system unique.

This article explains these variables as simply as possible, without mathematical overload.  
Unlike traditional stablecoins, ProjectUSD requires no:

- external USD peg  
- oracle feeds  
- banks  
- governance intervention  

Stability emerges **purely from internal feedback**.  
This feedback loop is generated by the interaction between **P**, **R** and **r**.

---

## 1. P – the market price

**P** is the actual trading price of ProjectUSD on PulseX.

- It is determined purely by supply and demand.  
- It can sit above or below the equilibrium price **R**.  
- It behaves like any other token price: volatile, fast, dynamic.

**In short:**  
**P = whatever the market does right now.**

The system does not judge P.  
It simply measures the deviation from R – and the controller reacts accordingly.

---

## 2. R – the internal equilibrium price

**R** is the value that the system defines as the “fair” internal reference price.

Important is what R is **not**:

- R does not come from a USD oracle.  
- R does not come from external prices like PLS or PLSX.  
- R is not set by teams, governance or humans.  
- R is not pegged to the dollar.  

Instead, R is a **purely internal, mathematically derived value** that emerges from the structure of the system itself.

R is the price at which ProjectUSD can always be **redeemed internally for PLS**.  
This redeemability creates a natural anchor and ensures that the market price P cannot drift away from the system value for long.

---

## 3. The price deviation ε

There is almost always a small difference between P and R.

This **relative** deviation is defined as:

**ε = (P − R) / R**

- ε > 0 → P is relatively above R (overvaluation)  
- ε < 0 → P is relatively below R (undervaluation)  

The magnitude of ε determines how strongly the controller adjusts r.

---

## 4. r – the system rate (corrective force)

**r** is the most important variable in the entire system.  
It is **not** interest, **not** a reward, **not** inflation, and **not** a governance parameter.

r is the **corrective force** that continuously pulls P back toward the equilibrium price R.

### When P > R  
- r increases  
- minting becomes more expensive  
- holding becomes less attractive  
- arbitrage and supply cool down the price  
→ **P moves downward toward R**

### When P < R  
- r decreases  
- holding becomes more attractive  
- arbitrage becomes profitable  
- demand increases  
→ **P moves upward toward R**

**In short:**  
**r is the automatic counter-force that corrects every deviation.**

---

## 5. Why R is not perfectly fixed – but extremely stable

R is not an absolutely fixed value.  
It can change – but:

- only slowly  
- never abruptly  
- never in response to external markets  
- only for internal, structural reasons  

The reason is simple: debt, collateral and redemptions change over time.  
To maintain long-term equilibrium, R must be able to adjust gently.

You can think of R as a **slowly drifting reference point**, while P is the fast, volatile market price.

---

## 6. How R is actually formed

R emerges from the **internal state of the system**, especially:

- the redemption mechanism  
- collateral and debt levels across all vaults  
- liquidation dynamics  
- internal drift mechanics  
- debt reduction and surplus-buffer effects  

This makes R a **book-accurate internal fair value**, unrelated to any external price feeds.

R is the value at which the system can always redeem ProjectUSD – guaranteed by mathematics, not by promises.

## 8. Summary

- **P** is volatile and reflects the market.  
- **R** is the internal equilibrium price – stable, slow-moving and systemic.  
- **r** is the automated corrective force that continuously pushes P back toward R.  

This is why ProjectUSD works:

- without a peg  
- without oracles  
- without governance  
- without USD  
- purely algorithmic  
- entirely on-chain  

**ProjectUSD stabilizes itself because r continuously drives P back toward R.**  
This creates an autonomous monetary system that enables a stable internal economy on PulseChain.
