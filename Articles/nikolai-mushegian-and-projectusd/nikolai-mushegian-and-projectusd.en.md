# Nikolai Mushegian’s RICO Concept and its Further Development in ProjectUSD

## Introduction

The history of algorithmic stablecoins can be divided into two major developmental paths.  
The first path begins with MakerDAO and the early DeFi systems. They proved that credit markets can function directly on chain, but over time they lost their independence through a growing reliance on fiat based collateral.

The second path is the lesser known but highly influential idea of Nikolai Mushegian. His concept RICO was meant to be a completely autonomous monetary system that stabilizes itself through its own internal dynamics without any reference to the US dollar. It was ahead of its time and remained unfinished, yet it contained the essential foundations of a truly autonomous value unit.

Today this idea reappears in a fully structured and technically complete form: **ProjectUSD**.

---

# 1 RICO in Brief  
The unfinished idea of an autonomous value unit

RICO was not intended as a product but as a vision of a monetary system that operates without external reference points of any kind.

Mushegian’s core idea:

**If a monetary system generates internal feedback, it can stabilize itself.**

The essential goals of RICO were:

* no fiat peg  
* no banks  
* no centralized reserves  
* no oracles  
* full autonomy through algorithmic dynamics

RICO represented the most radical form of an on chain value unit.  
But it never became a complete system. The architecture remained fragmentary, the mechanics incomplete, and the key variables were not yet fully defined.

---

# 2 ProjectUSD  
The technical and conceptual completion of the original RICO idea

ProjectUSD takes this idea and transforms it into a complete and functional system.  
The ProjectUSD Whitepaper V2.1 describes it as an autonomous feedback driven monetary design that operates entirely without oracles, banks or central control.

> ProjectUSD is a fully on chain algorithmic monetary system that achieves price stability without oracles banks or central intervention.

This means ProjectUSD achieves exactly what Mushegian intended with RICO, but for the first time in a closed, clear and verifiable form.

---

# 3 The Core Mechanism  
P, R and r as a feedback system

The key similarity between RICO and ProjectUSD is the idea that a value unit does not need to be fixed to anything external.  
Instead, it can stabilize itself through reactions to price deviations.

ProjectUSD formulates this as a feedback loop using three variables:

### P  
The free market price on the DEX.  
It simply reflects current market behaviour.

### R  
The internal equilibrium price.  
It acts as the system’s mathematical reference point and does not rely on any oracle.  
It is the value at which ProjectUSD can always be redeemed internally.

### r  
The system rate reacting to price deviations.  
It is the regulatory force of the entire system.

> r is the automatic counterforce that corrects every deviation.

This compact regulatory mechanism existed conceptually in RICO, but ProjectUSD is the first design to fully implement it.

---

# 4 The Architecture of ProjectUSD  
The missing technical framework that RICO never achieved

ProjectUSD is not only a concept but a complete infrastructure.  
The key components are defined in detail in the Whitepaper.

### Vaults  
PLS is deposited as collateral. New units of ProjectUSD are minted based on this collateral.

### Stability Pool  
The pool stabilizes the system by absorbing undercollateralized positions.

### Redemption Engine  
It ensures that the market price cannot drift away from the internal value for long.  
It serves as a fundamental value anchor without any oracle.

### Immutable Core  
A frozen core without any form of external modification.  
After the freeze event no team member or governance can change the system.

> What cannot be changed cannot be abused.

ProjectUSD is therefore the first implementation that technically realizes Mushegian’s philosophical principles.

---

# 5 How ProjectUSD advances the RICO idea

ProjectUSD goes beyond RICO in several crucial areas:

### 1 A clearly defined equilibrium price R  
RICO never offered a complete definition.  
ProjectUSD derives R as a mathematical internal system value independent of external markets.

### 2 A complete feedback function r  
The system rate r is determined strictly by the size of the deviation between P and R.  
It is bounded, transparent and algorithmic.

### 3 A fully functional minting process  
Vaults mint ProjectUSD based on PLS collateral.  
RICO only hinted at this mechanism.

### 4 A mature security model  
Median TWAP, outlier filtering, rate limits and anti MEV protections are all integrated.

### 5 A true autonomy system through the freeze event  
This transforms ProjectUSD into an organism without human intervention.

ProjectUSD is therefore not only a continuation of the idea but its actual technical realization.
