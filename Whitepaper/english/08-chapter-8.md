# Chapter 8 - Risks and Limitations: What ProjectUSD can and cannot do…

Every system that manages value carries risk within it.
The question is not **whether** risks exist,
but **where** they lie - and who controls them.

ProjectUSD can do many things:
It can create price stability without banks,
absorb market pressure,
and regulate itself.

But it cannot perform miracles.
And that honesty is, in itself, a form of strength.

---

### 8.1 Technical Risks

**Smart Contract Bugs:**
Even formal audits and verification cannot provide absolute guarantees.
A flaw in the core smart contract could still exist despite extensive testing.
Because the core becomes immutable after the freeze,
quality and verification must be uncompromising before deployment.

**MEV Manipulation and Network Stress:**
PulseChain offers high speed and low fees,
yet Miner Extractable Value (MEV) strategies remain possible.
ProjectUSD mitigates these risks through median oracles, TWAP mechanisms, and rate limiters -
but complete immunity does not exist.

**Oracle Bias:**
On-chain prices reflect liquidity and trading volume.
In periods of extreme illiquidity, the median TWAP may react sluggishly,
causing temporary deviations from equilibrium.

---

### 8.2 Economic Risks

**Collateral Volatility:**
ProjectUSD depends directly on the stability of its underlying PulseChain assets.
A sharp price drop in PLS or other collateral types
can trigger liquidation waves,
putting short-term pressure on the market price.

**Liquidity Shifts:**
If DEX trading volumes decline,
arbitrage between ProjectUSD and other assets slows.
The system remains functional,
but price deviations may persist longer.

**Psychological Factors:**
Markets are not machines.
During panic phases, people act irrationally -
selling too early, over-leveraging, or withdrawing liquidity.
Even a perfect algorithm cannot eliminate emotion.

---

### 8.3 Governance and Environmental Risks

**Governance Capture:**
Although the core is immutable,
the periphery - such as AMO or PSM parameters -
could theoretically be influenced by voting majorities.
Timelocks and transparent on-chain voting minimize this risk,
but cannot eliminate it entirely.

**Regulatory Uncertainty:**
ProjectUSD is not a regulated financial product.
In some jurisdictions, its use may have tax or legal implications.
Every user is responsible for complying with their local laws.

---

### 8.4 The Limits of the Model

ProjectUSD does not seek to replace the U.S. dollar.
It is not a fiat copy, but a **native digital unit of account**.
Its stability arises not from bank reserves or legal assurances,
but from the mathematical equilibrium of its mechanisms.

That means:
ProjectUSD is not a “digital dollar,”
but an independent measure of value and exchange within the PulseChain economy.
Those who understand this distinction will recognize the difference between **dependence and sovereignty**.

---

### 8.5 The Philosophical Truth of Risk

Perfect security does not exist -
but **honest systems** can make their risks transparent.
ProjectUSD transfers risk from people into code -
not to hide it,
but to make it visible, measurable, and fair.

Because in the end, **risk is not the opposite of trust - it is its foundation**.
Only those who understand where danger lies
can remain resilient over time.
