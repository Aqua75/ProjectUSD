# Chapter 8 - Risks and Limitations: What ProjectUSD can and cannot do…

Every system that manages value carries risk within it.<br>
The question is not **whether** risks exist,<br>
but **where** they lie - and who controls them.

ProjectUSD can do many things:<br>
It can create price stability without banks,<br>
absorb market pressure,<br>
and regulate itself.

But it cannot perform miracles.<br>
And that honesty is, in itself, a form of strength.

---

### 8.1 Technical Risks

**Smart Contract Bugs:** <br>
Even formal audits and verification cannot provide absolute guarantees.<br>
A flaw in the core smart contract could still exist despite extensive testing.<br>
Because the core becomes immutable after the freeze,<br>
quality and verification must be uncompromising before deployment.

**MEV Manipulation and Network Stress:** <br>
PulseChain offers high speed and low fees,<br>
yet Miner Extractable Value (MEV) strategies remain possible.<br>
ProjectUSD mitigates these risks through median oracles, TWAP mechanisms, and rate limiters -<br>
but complete immunity does not exist.

**Oracle Bias:** <br>
On-chain prices reflect liquidity and trading volume.<br>
In periods of extreme illiquidity, the median TWAP may react sluggishly,<br>
causing temporary deviations from equilibrium.

---

### 8.2 Economic Risks

**Collateral Volatility:** <br>
ProjectUSD depends directly on the stability of its underlying PulseChain assets.<br>
A sharp price drop in PLS or other collateral types<br>
can trigger liquidation waves,<br>
putting short-term pressure on the market price.

**Liquidity Shifts:** <br>
If DEX trading volumes decline,<br>
arbitrage between ProjectUSD and other assets slows.<br>
The system remains functional,<br>
but price deviations may persist longer.

**Psychological Factors:** <br>
Markets are not machines.<br>
During panic phases, people act irrationally -<br>
selling too early, over-leveraging, or withdrawing liquidity.<br>
Even a perfect algorithm cannot eliminate emotion.

---

### 8.3 Governance and Environmental Risks

**Governance Capture:** <br>
Although the core is immutable,<br>
the periphery - such as AMO or PSM parameters -<br>
could theoretically be influenced by voting majorities.<br>
Timelocks and transparent on-chain voting minimize this risk,<br>
but cannot eliminate it entirely.

**Regulatory Uncertainty:** <br>
ProjectUSD is not a regulated financial product.<br>
In some jurisdictions, its use may have tax or legal implications.<br>
Every user is responsible for complying with their local laws.

---

### 8.4 The Limits of the Model

ProjectUSD does not seek to replace the U.S. dollar.<br>
It is not a fiat copy, but a **native digital unit of account**.<br>
Its stability arises not from bank reserves or legal assurances,<br>
but from the mathematical equilibrium of its mechanisms.

That means:<br>
ProjectUSD is not a “digital dollar,”<br>
but an independent measure of value and exchange within the PulseChain economy.<br>
Those who understand this distinction will recognize the difference between **dependence and<br>
sovereignty**.

---

### 8.5 The Philosophical Truth of Risk

Perfect security does not exist -<br>
but **honest systems** can make their risks transparent.<br>
ProjectUSD transfers risk from people into code -<br>
not to hide it,<br>
but to make it visible, measurable, and fair.

Because in the end, **risk is not the opposite of trust - it is its foundation**.<br>
Only those who understand where danger lies<br>
can remain resilient over time.
