## Chapter 2 - What Is ProjectUSD?

ProjectUSD is not just another stablecoin in the endless stream of new tokens.

It is an attempt to redefine money - without oracles, without banks, without reliance on people or institutions.

A system that finds its own balance because it understands its own rules.

The core idea is simple:

Stability can emerge when a system responds to itself.

When supply and demand, debt and savings, expansion and contraction communicate within a closed loop - purely mathematically, without any external data source.

At the heart of this system lies the internal equilibrium price R.

It is the inner compass by which the market price P is guided.

When the actual market price on decentralized exchanges deviates from R,

the system automatically responds through a variable r - the combined debt and savings rate.

r acts as the pulse of the economy:

when r rises, minting new tokens becomes less attractive;

when r falls, holding or generating ProjectUSD becomes more appealing.

In this way, the price continually gravitates back toward its inner anchor, R.

This equilibrium arises without oracles -

without anyone declaring from outside what “one dollar” should be worth.

Value emerges organically from the interaction of participants:

from Vaults that provide collateral,

from Stability Pools that absorb weak positions,

and from the Redemption Engine that allows anyone to redeem ProjectUSD for PLS at the internal price.

Three simple components, one closed cycle:

Mint → Circulate → Redeem → Burn → Mint again.

Everything happens on-chain - verifiable, transparent, and autonomous.

Thus, ProjectUSD is not merely a technical design but a living economic ecosystem.

It behaves like a miniature central bank - only without people, without politics, without power structures.

Instead of promises, there is code.

Instead of trust, there is transparency.

And instead of a forced peg to the U.S. dollar, there exists an internal, algorithmically defined measure of value.

The result is a self-sustaining equilibrium system -

one that draws its stability from motion,

like a planet that remains steady not because it stands still,

but because it moves in perfect orbit.
