# Chapter 6 - The Path to Autonomy: Roadmap and Development Phases

ProjectUSD is not an idea for a quick token launch.
It is an infrastructure vision - a stable nervous system for the entire PulseChain economy.
For a system designed to regulate itself to truly function, its launch must happen **step by step**.
Each phase is both a test and a proof - a demonstration that stability can be maintained without intervention.

---

### 6.1 Phase 1 - Guarded Launch

The beginning is deliberately small.
During the Guarded Launch phase, strict limits apply:

- Low maximum debt ceiling,
- No active PSM or AMO modules,
- Intensive monitoring of all on-chain metrics.

The goal is to measure, under real market conditions,
how efficiently the Controller responds to price deviations,
how well the Stability Pool absorbs stress periods,
and how quickly the market price stabilizes around **R**.

All data is public.
Every metric can be viewed in real time -
an open laboratory on PulseChain.

Only after ProjectUSD proves sustained stability over several weeks
does the transition to Phase 2 begin.

---

### 6.2 Phase 2 - Parameter Freeze and Proof of Stability

Once equilibrium has been consistently demonstrated -
peg deviations remain small, liquidations are efficient, collateral ratios are healthy -
the core parameters are frozen.

This means:

- The Collateral Ratio, liquidation threshold, Controller logic, and Redemption mechanism become permanently fixed.
- The ProjectUSD core becomes **immutable**.

From that moment, ProjectUSD is no longer an experiment
but an independent, living organism.
No governance vote, no developer update can alter it.
Code becomes law - and the law endures.

---

### 6.3 Phase 3 - Peg Stability Module (PSM Light)

Only after the successful freeze can the Peg Stability Basket optionally be activated.
This basket contains other on-chain stablecoins
that serve as limited friction dampeners within strict boundaries.

The rules remain tight:

- Daily limit per slot (e.g., 1% of total supply),
- Small swap haircuts (0.1-0.5%),
- Full on-chain logging and auditability.

The PSM’s purpose is not to prop up the peg but to smooth it.
Even if the PSM fails, ProjectUSD remains fully functional.

---

### 6.4 Phase 4 - Algorithmic Market Operations (AMO On)

After prolonged stability, controlled AMO modules can be activated.
These operate within narrow price ranges,
provide liquidity, or exploit arbitrage spreads
to optimize the Surplus Buffer.

Each AMO operates under a fixed budget and is fully auditable.
They act not in the shadows, but under the watchful eye of the blockchain.

This allows ProjectUSD to grow organically into DeFi markets
without exposing itself to centralized interference.

---

### 6.5 Phase 5 - Integration into the PulseChain Ecosystem

Once ProjectUSD has proven itself as a stable value anchor,
it begins integration into the broader PulseChain network:

- **DEX pairs:** ProjectUSD/PLS, ProjectUSD/PLSX, ProjectUSD/INC
- **Lending protocols:** ProjectUSD as collateral and borrowable asset
- **Derivatives & perpetuals:** ProjectUSD as margin and settlement currency
- **Payment gateways:** ProjectUSD as a neutral unit of account

With each integration, the network effect grows.
The more protocols adopt ProjectUSD as their base currency,
the more independent PulseChain becomes from centralized stablecoins.

---

### 6.6 Phase 6 - Long-Term Evolution

Once established in the market, ProjectUSD can evolve organically:

- Addition of more native collateral types,
- Introduction of a long-term savings rate (DSR) funded by the Surplus Buffer,
- Decentralized governance of AMO parameters,
- Synergies with PulseX, LiquidLoans, and other ecosystem projects.

Over time, ProjectUSD could become the **unit of account for the entire PulseChain economy** -
not as a dollar substitute, but as its own mathematically defined currency.

---

### 6.7 A Philosophy of Slow Growth

ProjectUSD follows a simple credo:

“Code first, hype never.”

Each phase begins only when the previous one is proven stable.
Trust is not built through promises, but through observation.
The goal is not short-term TVL success,
but a lasting foundation for decentralized money.
