## Abstract

Money traditionally fulfills three functions: medium of exchange, store of value and unit of account. While the first two functions can emerge relatively quickly in crypto economies, the unit of account is the most demanding and structurally decisive function. Whoever provides the unit of account effectively defines how value is measured, compared and accounted for.

This study examines under which conditions ProjectUSD can become the natural unit of account within the PulseChain economy. “Natural” does not imply decree or UI enforcement, but spontaneous, coordinated adoption by market participants: prices, contracts, balance sheets, risk metrics and mental price anchors are expressed by default in ProjectUSD.

Building on monetary theory, network and adoption models and the ProjectUSD mechanisms described in the whitepaper, this study develops a macroeconomic threshold framework. The objective is to clarify when and why the transition from a stable asset to a dominant unit of account occurs, and which roles stability, liquidity, credibility and integration play in this process.

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Why the unit of account is the hardest monetary function

Money fulfills three core functions:

- medium of exchange  
- store of value  
- unit of account  

In crypto economies, media of exchange emerge easily because tokens are immediately transferable. The store of value function can also develop through narratives, supply constraints or collateral mechanisms.

The unit of account, however, represents a coordination problem. Prices, contracts, accounting systems, margin models and mental price anchors must converge on a common measure. This coordination is social, institutional and psychological in nature.

As a result, the unit of account is usually the last function to emerge, yet the one that structures an economy most deeply.

## 1.2 The problem of current DeFi units of account

In many DeFi systems, centralized stablecoins such as USDC or USDT function as de facto numeraires. While they offer short-term price stability, they introduce external dependencies:

- banks and off-chain reserves  
- jurisdictions and regulatory access points  
- blacklisting and freeze risks  

These dependencies conflict with the ideal of a fully autonomous on-chain economy.

ProjectUSD addresses this issue conceptually. It is designed as a fully on-chain monetary system that seeks to generate price stability through mechanisms and feedback rather than external guarantees. The whitepaper explicitly emphasizes stability without banks, without off-chain oracles and without discretionary intervention.

## 1.3 Research question

The central question of this study is:

When does ProjectUSD become the natural unit of account?

“Natural” means that ProjectUSD becomes the unit of account when participants in the PulseChain economy spontaneously and by default use it to:

1. quote prices  
2. maintain balance sheets  
3. denominate contracts

5. anchor value mentally  

This study provides a macroeconomic framework for answering this question.

# 2. The unit of account in economies

## 2.1 The unit of account as numéraire

In economic theory, the unit of account is the numéraire, the reference in which all other prices are expressed. Formally, almost any good can serve this role. In practice, however, the unit of account that prevails is the one that is:

- relatively stable  
- highly liquid  
- broadly accepted  
- suitable for contracts  

The unit of account is therefore less a technical property than a social convention reinforced by strong network effects.

## 2.2 Why price stability is central to the unit of account

An economy can function with volatile assets as media of exchange if prices are continuously renegotiated. This is not true for the unit of account.

Volatility creates:

- menu costs  
- accounting friction  
- distorted incentives in credit and derivatives markets  

As a result, the dominant unit of account is typically the asset that acts as a stability anchor.

## 2.3 Vehicle currency logic

Historically, dominant units of account often emerge through a sequence:

1. liquidity  
2. settlement usage  
3. credit and derivatives  
4. price quotation  
5. accounting and mental anchoring  

In on-chain economies, this sequence is particularly visible because DEXs, lending protocols and perpetuals explicitly enforce quote and settlement standards.

## 2.4 On-chain specificity: the unit of account as measurement infrastructure

In DeFi, the unit of account is not only a mental convention, but also embedded in:

- price oracles  
- risk engines  
- collateral parameters  
- liquidation logic  
- UI defaults  

Whoever becomes the unit of account shapes the measurement instruments of the entire economy. ProjectUSD explicitly addresses this through full on-chain transparency of its core metrics.

# 3. Adoption models

## 3.1 Network effects and path dependence

Currency competition is characterized by increasing returns to scale. The usefulness of a unit of account grows with its adoption, leading to path dependence:

- early standards exhibit inertia  
- switching incurs coordination costs  
- agents quote where others quote  

Superior design wins only if critical thresholds are crossed.

## 3.2 Coordination games and multiple equilibria

Units of account form coordination equilibria. Multiple stable outcomes are possible, such as:

- most prices are denominated in USDC  
- most prices are denominated in ProjectUSD  

Transitions typically occur through shocks or institutional standardization.

## 3.3 Diffusion dynamics

Adoption often follows an S-curve:

1. innovators  
2. early adopters  
3. on-chain mainstream  
4. late majority  

For ProjectUSD, the transition from phase two to phase three is decisive.

## 3.4 Two-sided market dynamics

Stablecoin adoption constitutes a two-sided market:

- users demand liquidity  
- liquidity providers demand volume and stability  
- protocols demand reliable units of account  

This creates a feedback loop:

More usage → more liquidity → better price quality → higher trust → more usage

ProjectUSD is explicitly designed as a feedback system in which the market price P oscillates around the internal reference price R, governed by the system rate r.

## 3.5 Observable indicators of unit of account adoption

The transition to a unit of account can be empirically observed through:

**Price quotation**  
- share of DEX pairs using ProjectUSD as the quote asset  
- default pricing in user interfaces  

**Contracts**  
- lending and derivatives denominated in ProjectUSD  

**Accounting**  
- DAO treasuries budgeted in ProjectUSD  

**Stability metrics**  
- deviation |P - R|  
- duration of peg deviations  
- market depth around R

2. liquidity dominance
